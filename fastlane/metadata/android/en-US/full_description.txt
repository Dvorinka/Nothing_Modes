Nothing Modes is an open-source Android automation app for Nothing phones.

Modes persist and restore. Routines fire on real events. Glyph lights respond. One open app, no cloud, no telemetry.

- Modes: Sleep, Work, Gaming. A mode holds a set of toggles and reverts every one when it ends.
- Routines: triggers for time windows, boot, battery, screen, notifications, calls, SMS, connectivity, app opens, geofences, and more.
- Conditions: gate execution with AND logic.
- Actions: 37+ types, from toggles and notifications to Glyph patterns and Shizuku-backed system changes.
- Glyph: drive zones, the Glyph Matrix, and Glyph Toys from any routine.
- Shizuku: airplane mode, hotspot, NFC and more. Without it the app degrades gracefully.

GPL-3.0.

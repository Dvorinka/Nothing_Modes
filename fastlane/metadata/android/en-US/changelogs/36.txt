- Fixed a crash on Android 12+ where a background-delivered event (battery, screen, connectivity) could kill the app with ForegroundServiceStartNotAllowedException.
- The persistent monitor now registers its receivers only after the service is fully foreground, closing a denial window that could crash during startup or restart.
- Widget refreshes and agent control broadcasts can no longer be cut short by process reclamation mid-write.
- Automation engine service lifecycle hardened: foreground-start denials are handled gracefully, and in-flight triggers are tracked so the service never stops mid-event.
